Directions

Part B · Critical Reasoning

Question → Design → Result → Limit → Claim

Questions 11–15
11Difficult
Ecologists surveyed sixty city ponds and found that ponds with denser shoreline vegetation tended to contain more frog species. The ponds also differed in size, water quality, and distance from roads, and the researchers did not alter any pond conditions.

Which choice most logically follows from the text?

  1. A. In the surveyed ponds, denser shoreline vegetation was associated with greater frog diversity, though other pond features may have contributed to the pattern.
  2. B. Dense shoreline vegetation alone caused the surveyed ponds to support more frog species.
  3. C. Every city pond would support more frog species if managers increased its shoreline vegetation.
  4. D. Ponds with more frog species caused denser shoreline vegetation to develop.
12Difficult
Researchers randomly assigned genetically similar basil seedlings to two growth chambers. Both chambers had the same temperature, water schedule, and light intensity, but one used red light and the other used white light. After four weeks, seedlings under red light were taller on average. Only one basil variety was tested.

Which choice states the most strongly supported conclusion?

  1. A. Red light will make every variety of basil grow taller than white light does.
  2. B. White light generally prevents basil seedlings from reaching their normal height.
  3. C. The tested basil variety would have grown taller under red light in any environment.
  4. D. Under the tested conditions, red light increased average seedling height relative to white light, but the result may not extend to other basil varieties.
13Difficult
One history class used a digital note-taking system, while another class used printed note packets. The digital-note class later earned higher quiz scores. However, that class also met for fifteen additional minutes each week, and students were not randomly assigned to the two classes.

Which choice best identifies a limitation on the conclusion that note format caused the score difference?

  1. A. The study did not examine whether printed note packets are easier for adults to store.
  2. B. The design cannot separate the effect of note format from differences in instructional time or preexisting class composition.
  3. C. Digital notes cannot affect quiz performance unless every student uses the same device.
  4. D. The higher-scoring class must have received more difficult quiz questions.
14Moderate
A temperature logger records one reading every fifteen minutes. In the test chamber, some heat spikes last fewer than five minutes. A second sensor records continuously. Because a brief spike can begin and end between two readings from the logger, ______.

Which choice most logically completes the text?

  1. A. the logger's fifteen-minute schedule creates the chamber's brief heat spikes
  2. B. the continuously recording sensor is necessarily less precise than the logger
  3. C. the continuously recording sensor may capture heat spikes that the logger misses
  4. D. researchers should stop using interval-based instruments in every experiment
15Difficult
Ornithologists observed migrating birds at two coastal sites. On windier nights, fewer birds departed from the northern site, but departure rates at the southern site changed little. The sites differed in available food, tree cover, and distance from artificial lights, and the study did not manipulate wind conditions.

Which choice most logically follows from the text?

  1. A. Wind conditions may be related to departure behavior at the northern site, but the observations do not establish wind as the sole cause of the difference between sites.
  2. B. Strong winds caused birds at the northern site to remain because that site had less available food.
  3. C. Artificial light explains why departure rates at the southern site remained stable.
  4. D. Migrating birds generally ignore wind when they have access to sufficient tree cover.
