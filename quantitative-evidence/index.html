1.3 Evidence Directions

DirectionWhat the Data Must DoCore CheckDemo
Complete / Illustrate用相关数据把句子或例子补完整。Does the data instantiate the exact idea before the blank?
Support让 claim 更可信；数据方向与预测一致。If this evidence is true, does belief in the claim increase?
Undermine / Weaken显示相反结果、无效果或关键例外。Would the claim need revision because of this evidence?

1.4 Common Data Relationships

RelationshipSafe LanguageBoundaryDemo
One Value“Group A was about 42%.”Check group, measure, unit, and time.
Difference“A was higher than B by about 8 points.”Percentage points and percent are not interchangeable.
Change over Time“A increased from 20 to 31.”Do not confuse change in one group with difference between groups.
Positive Correlation“As X increased, Y tended to increase.”Correlation does not prove that X caused Y.
Negative Correlation“As X increased, Y tended to decrease.”Check the whole pattern, not two convenient points.
No Stable Pattern“The values varied without a consistent direction.”Do not force a trend that the display does not show.

1.5 Precision Language

Exact vs. Approximate

图中未标出精确值时，用 about / approximately / nearly。

Strength

Association 不能自动支持 always, proves, causes, all。

Respectively

第一组对应第一个值，第二组对应第二个值。

Prediction

只有题目要求时才延伸趋势；预测必须在可见模式内。

1.6 Trap Taxonomy

Accurate but Off-Claim

数字是真的，但没有完成 claim。

Wrong Direction

把 increase 写成 decrease，或把 support 写成 undermine。

Wrong Field

换了 group、measure、time、condition 或 comparison。

Right Idea, Wrong Magnitude

方向正确，但数值或差值不匹配。

Overclaim

把 association 写成 cause，或把 limited sample 推到全部人群。

Background Knowledge

用常识替代 display 中实际给出的证据。

3Method System

3.1 Overall Method — Claim-Direction-Evidence Lock

Box the Claim框出真正要证明、举例或削弱的句子。
Label Direction写 C / S / U。
Predict Evidence先预测什么结果会完成这个 job。
Test Variables逐项检查 group, measure, condition, direction。
Keep Exact Fit选择最直接、最完整、不过度的证据。

3.2 Subtype Method — Claim-to-Cells Map

Claim First标出 group, measure, comparison, condition, direction, magnitude。
Frame确认 title, axes/headers, units, legend, scale。
Points只定位 claim 需要的 cells / points。
Sentence先说出准确的 English numeric comparison。
Match + Limit匹配 wording、strength 与 evidence boundary。

4Overall-Method Worked Micro-Model

WORKED MINI-QUESTION

SAT task: Use the table to select the evidence that most effectively supports the claim.

Claim: “The treatment group’s advantage over the control group grew from 2022 to 2023.”

YearControlTreatment
20224244
20234351

Which choice most effectively uses data from the table to support the claim?

  1. A.The treatment group had a value of 51 in 2023.
  2. B.The control group increased from 42 in 2022 to 43 in 2023.
  3. C.The treatment group exceeded the control group by 2 units in 2022 and by 8 units in 2023.
  4. D.The treatment group increased by 7 units, while the control group increased by 1 unit.

Model resolution: C

ClaimAdvantage grew
DirectionSupport
Cells44−42=2; 51−43=8
MatchGap grew from 2 to 8

Why C: “Advantage grew” requires both treatment–control gaps. C states both comparisons directly.

Why A is tempting: 51 is accurate, but one value cannot show that the advantage changed across two years.

5Subtype Reference & Applications

5.1 Source-Grounded Annotated Demonstration

ANNOTATED MINI-QUESTION

SAT task: Use the table to select the example that most effectively illustrates the statement.

Statement: An antibiotic can be effective against more than 80% of infections generally yet be over 20 percentage points less effective against MRSA.

AntibioticGeneral effectivenessMRSA effectiveness
Vancomycin100%100%
Mupirocin90.28%73.08%
Rifampicin86.11%61.54%

Which choice most effectively uses data from the table to illustrate the statement?

  1. A.Vancomycin was 100% effective both generally and against MRSA.
  2. B.Mupirocin was 90.28% effective generally and 73.08% effective against MRSA.
  3. C.Rifampicin was 86.11% effective generally but only 61.54% effective against MRSA.
  4. D.Rifampicin was 61.54% effective generally and 86.11% effective against MRSA.

Annotated resolution: C

Claim-to-Cells Map: same antibiotic → general effectiveness above 80% → MRSA effectiveness more than 20 points lower.

Calculation: 86.11 − 61.54 = 24.57 percentage points.

Closest trap: B. Its values are accurate, but the gap is only 17.20 percentage points, so it misses the “over 20” field.
